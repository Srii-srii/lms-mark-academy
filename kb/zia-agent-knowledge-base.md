## Course: ECG Interpretation Essentials

- Course ID: ecg-101
- Topic: Reading ECGs and heart rhythms
- Level: Beginner
- Duration: 6 hours over 12 lessons
- Price: ₹1,499 (INR)
- Certificate: yes, on completion
- Course page: https://academy.lms-mark.website/course.html?id=ecg-101

What it covers: Read rhythm strips with confidence: rate, rhythm, axis, intervals and the ten patterns you will meet most.

Who ECG Interpretation Essentials is for: Nursing staff, junior doctors, paramedics and students who see ECGs on shift but don't yet read them confidently.

What learners learn in ECG Interpretation Essentials:

- Systematic 7-step ECG read
- Recognise common arrhythmias
- Spot STEMI patterns early

If ECG Interpretation Essentials is not the right fit, suggest:

- Basic Life Support Refresher (All levels, ₹799) when you want hands-on emergency skills rather than interpretation.
- Clinical Pharmacology Basics (Intermediate, ₹1,999) when you already read ECGs and want to understand the cardiac drugs behind them.

## Course: Basic Life Support Refresher

- Course ID: bls-201
- Topic: Emergencies and resuscitation
- Level: All levels
- Duration: 3 hours over 8 lessons
- Price: ₹799 (INR)
- Certificate: yes, on completion
- Course page: https://academy.lms-mark.website/course.html?id=bls-201

What it covers: Current adult, child and infant BLS sequences, team roles and high-quality CPR metrics.

Who Basic Life Support Refresher is for: Any healthcare worker who needs to refresh resuscitation skills, from students to senior staff.

What learners learn in Basic Life Support Refresher:

- High-quality CPR metrics
- AED use
- Team-based resuscitation

If Basic Life Support Refresher is not the right fit, suggest:

- ECG Interpretation Essentials (Beginner, ₹1,499) when you already know BLS well and want the next step: recognising dangerous rhythms.
- Clinical Pharmacology Basics (Intermediate, ₹1,999) when you want ward knowledge rather than emergency skills.

## Course: Clinical Pharmacology Basics

- Course ID: pharm-110
- Topic: Medicines and safe prescribing
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 8 hours over 16 lessons
- Price: ₹1,999 (INR)
- Certificate: yes, on completion
- Course page: https://academy.lms-mark.website/course.html?id=pharm-110

What it covers: Pharmacokinetics, dosing in renal and hepatic impairment, and the interactions that matter on the ward.

Who Clinical Pharmacology Basics is for: Nurses, pharmacists and doctors who prescribe, check or give medicines on the ward.

What learners learn in Clinical Pharmacology Basics:

- PK/PD fundamentals
- Dose adjustment
- High-risk interactions

If Clinical Pharmacology Basics is not the right fit, suggest:

- Basic Life Support Refresher (All levels, ₹799) when you want a short, practical course you can finish in one sitting.
- Medical Research Writing (Advanced, ₹1,299) when you're more interested in evidence and publishing than day-to-day prescribing.

## Course: Medical Research Writing

- Course ID: research-300
- Topic: Research and publishing
- Level: Advanced
- Duration: 5 hours over 10 lessons
- Price: ₹1,299 (INR)
- Certificate: yes, on completion
- Course page: https://academy.lms-mark.website/course.html?id=research-300

What it covers: Structure, write and submit a clinical paper: IMRaD, reporting guidelines and responding to reviewers.

Who Medical Research Writing is for: Clinicians and postgraduate students planning to write up a case, audit or study for publication.

What learners learn in Medical Research Writing:

- IMRaD structure
- Reporting checklists
- Reviewer responses

If Medical Research Writing is not the right fit, suggest:

- Clinical Pharmacology Basics (Intermediate, ₹1,999) when you want clinical knowledge you can use on shift rather than writing skills.
- ECG Interpretation Essentials (Beginner, ₹1,499) when you want a beginner-friendly clinical skill with quick wins.
