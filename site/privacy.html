Privacy and consent

Demo notice for an internal proof of concept. Must be reviewed by legal before any real use.

What we collect

Your name, email address and (optionally) mobile number when you join the academy or chat with us, plus the pages and courses you view if you accept analytics cookies.

Why we collect it

  • Service messages: enrolment confirmations, receipts and certificates for actions you take. These are sent whether or not you opt in to marketing.
  • Marketing: course announcements, webinars and offers, only on the channels you tick (email, WhatsApp, SMS).
  • Support: answering questions you send through chat, Messenger, Instagram or WhatsApp.

Your choices

  • Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp or SMS marketing message.
  • Change cookie choices any time from Cookie settings in the footer.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your data through the chat.

Who processes it

We use Zoho (Marketing Automation, SalesIQ, CPaaS and Flow) to store contacts and send messages, and Meta for WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook messaging.
